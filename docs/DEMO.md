# 3-minute demo

1. **Problem (20 s).** Incomplete applications cause back-and-forth with City staff and delays, and applicants don't know which requirements apply to them.
2. **Preview (30 s).** Click Start, type "backyard porch" and press Enter (it finds "Deck or porch"). On Preview, enter a height of 0.4 m with no roof: **"A permit may not be required"**, and the flow stops there. Change the height to 0.9 m: a permit is likely required, so continue.
3. **One PDF per page (60 s).** Home → load the *Residential deck* sample. Each document has its own page: the application form, site plan, deck plan and side view. Every field is checked automatically: application details from what you typed, and drawings from the uploaded PDF. On the deck plan, "Beam size: Not found" says where to add it, and **Show me where** boxes the bare "BEAM" label. Click Next: because items are unfinished, it asks before continuing.
4. **Declaration (15 s).** The required declaration from Section I of the Ontario form. The signature itself belongs on the PDF, and the reviewer found none on this sample.
5. **Summary and confirmation (30 s).** The summary shows **Application incomplete** with the six InfoPkg items grouped by document. Click one to jump straight to that page. Download the PDF summary. The confirmation page says "Application incomplete". Then load *Complete deck (control)* and it says **Application appears complete**, with no false positives.
6. **Wrong files (15 s).** On the *Detached garage* sample, upload `fixtures/files/FILE-002/ProtectedPlan.pdf` on the floor plan page ("Upload an unsecured version of the PDF.") and `FILE-006/GaragePlan.pdf` on the site plan page (address mismatch, with both addresses shown).
7. **Close (10 s).** This is preliminary guidance only: it supports City review and never replaces it.
