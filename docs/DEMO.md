# 3-minute demo

1. **Problem (20 s).** Incomplete applications cause back-and-forth with City staff and delays, and applicants don't know which requirements apply to them.
2. **New project (40 s).** Click Start, type "backyard porch" and press Enter (it finds "Deck or porch"). Fill in the application details. On Requirements & uploads, enter a height of 0.4 m with no roof: **"A permit may not be required"**, and no uploads are requested. Change the height to 0.9 m and the checklist appears, with each requirement on the left and its own upload on the right, plus where to add each item and links to City guidance.
3. **Review real drawings (60 s).** Home → load the *Residential deck* sample → Check my documents. The result is **Action required**, with the six InfoPkg messages (signature, property lines, shed, beam, joists, side view). Open "Beam information is incomplete" and click **Show me where**: the bare "BEAM" label on the deck plan is boxed, with where to add the size.
4. **Control (15 s).** Load *Complete deck (control)*: **Appears ready to submit**, with zero findings and no false positives.
5. **Wrong or inconsistent files (25 s).** Load *Detached garage* and add `fixtures/files/FILE-002/ProtectedPlan.pdf` and `FILE-006/GaragePlan.pdf` under "Other supporting documents". You get "Upload an unsecured version of the PDF." and an address mismatch with both addresses side by side.
6. **PDF (10 s).** Continue to the summary and click **Download PDF summary**. It has the application details at the top and a table of each missing item with where to add it and a clickable guidance link.
7. **Close (10 s).** Turn on high contrast mode. This is preliminary guidance only: it supports City review and never replaces it.
